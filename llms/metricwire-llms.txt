# Metricwire

> Metricwire is a real-world data collection platform for ecological momentary assessment (EMA), experience sampling (ESM), and digital-health research. Organizations design studies, schedule time- and event-based prompts, and collect participant self-reports alongside passive sensor and wearable data through native iOS and Android apps, then export results for analysis. Used across clinical trials, behavioral and public-health research, and patient-reported outcome programs, with an emphasis on data privacy and security (ISO 27001, GDPR).
